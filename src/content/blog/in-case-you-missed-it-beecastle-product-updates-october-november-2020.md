We’ve listened to your feedback and have been hard at work to make BeeCastle more powerful, easier to manage, and even faster to use.

Here’s what we’ve been working on…

## New User Analytics Dashboard

We have launched version 1 of our highly requested User Analytics dashboard. It’s designed to look inwards at your organisation for a more granular view of how your team is engaging.

This release displays some basic analytics - but further functionality and deeper insights are on the way in the coming weeks - keep an eye out!

To see version 1 in action click [here](https://app.beecastle.com/reporting/activity/team-dashboard).

## Sign-in with Microsoft

New and existing users can now sign-in to BeeCastle with their Microsoft account details. On-boarding is much easier as you no longer need to manage multiple passwords. Any security policies you’ve applied through Microsoft 365 will now apply when you sign in to BeeCastle.

![](../../assets/blog/in-case-you-missed-it-beecastle-product-updates-october-november-2020/beecastle-sign-in.png)

We suggest transferring your account over to sign in with Microsoft 365, to do this simply select ‘Sign in with Microsoft’ and follow the prompts.

For a full feature overview you can read more [here](/blog/product-update-sign-in-via-microsoft-365/ "Here").

## Load your data faster

We’ve been working hard on optimising our platform so that you can get into your data quicker. We’ve made improvements across all BeeCastle dashboards which means quicker loading times, smoother scrolling and a faster filtering experience.
